POST (copy, adjust to taste)

I think we should stop saying "DCB" and say what we mean.

When people ask for it, they usually want three things:
1. model events without being forced into one aggregate per stream
2. one clear owner for who produces an event
3. rules decided per command, not per entity

In Chronicle each of those has a plain mechanism: event source definitions, constraints at append, decision reads on a command, and aggregates where an aggregate is the better fit. Validators still do input validation and nothing more.

I also went back over what I wrote in March. Two things I got wrong, and the post says so.

The post has the code in C#, Kotlin, Java, TypeScript and Elixir where the client supports it, and a table of where they differ.
